At the forest's edge the birdsong thinned, then stopped. The path remained plain enough beneath my feet, but the trees seemed to lean close over it, knotting their branches together until the afternoon became green dusk.

I kept to the marked track and stopped often to listen. Wind moved somewhere high above, though the ferns around me did not stir. Once, a branch cracked behind me. I turned to find only the long empty path and my own footprints pressed into the damp soil.

The far side opened suddenly into sunlit grass. I sat there until my hands stopped gripping the walking stick, then made a note to return another day with company, a compass, and rather better sense.
